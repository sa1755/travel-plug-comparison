# TravelPlug affiliate recommendations

TravelPlug is affiliate-ready but does not currently publish any products or
affiliate links. The public catalog in `src/data/adapter-products.ts` is empty by
design. Electrical compatibility is always calculated before this optional
product layer runs.

## Trust and recommendation rules

- Recommend an adapter only when the comparison engine says a physical adapter
  may be needed.
- Match products using origin plugs, destination sockets, and any explicit
  origin/destination country restrictions.
- Keep the practical electrical answer and device guidance above product cards.
- Add only products reviewed for the claimed route. Do not infer voltage
  conversion from an adapter listing.
- Never invent URLs, availability, prices, ratings, endorsements, or retailer
  approval.

## Adding a genuine product

Add a typed record to `src/data/adapter-products.ts` with:

- a stable internal `id`;
- truthful `name`, `description`, and `retailer` values;
- the retailer's canonical `productUrl`;
- an optional legitimate `affiliateUrl` generated by the approved affiliate
  account;
- `affiliateProgramme` and the programme-approved `affiliateDisclosure` whenever
  an affiliate URL is present;
- matching `originPlugTypes` and `destinationPlugTypes`;
- optional `supportedOriginCountries` and
  `supportedDestinationCountries` route restrictions;
- `active: true` only after review; and
- a `priority` used solely to order otherwise valid matches.

Use test-only fixtures in the recommendation-service tests while developing.
Do not commit placeholder products to the public catalog.

## Display and measurement

`findAdapterRecommendations()` filters the catalog after the comparison result
has established that an adapter is relevant. The product component stays hidden
when there are no valid products, marks genuine affiliate destinations with
`rel="sponsored"`, places the disclosure beside the recommendation, and records
`adapter_recommendation_viewed` and `adapter_product_clicked` using bounded
product, retailer, route, and adapter-type identifiers.

A click is not a sale. Sales and commission reporting must come from the
retailer or affiliate network.

## Amazon Associates

The same data model can hold legitimate Amazon Associates links, but account
creation and approval are intentionally outside the codebase. Before adding an
Amazon link:

1. Create or complete the user's Amazon Associates account.
2. Obtain the approved tracking ID for the relevant storefront.
3. Generate the product link using Amazon's approved tools.
4. Review the current Associates Operating Agreement and disclosure rules.
5. Add any exact Amazon-required disclosure copy beside the links in addition
   to the existing general affiliate disclosure.
6. Recheck the product and route match before activating it.

Do not scrape Amazon, copy restricted product content, hardcode prices, or claim
Amazon approval without current evidence.
