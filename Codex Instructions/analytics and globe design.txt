============================================================

to:

The product message should remain extremely simple.

TravelPlug's core purpose:

A traveller enters their current country and destination country.

TravelPlug tells them, in plain English:

1. Whether they need a plug adapter.
2. Whether they need a voltage converter.
3. Whether their devices are likely to work.
4. Why.
