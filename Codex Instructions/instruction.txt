------------------------------------------------------------

PRIMARY PRODUCT GOAL

The website exists to answer one question.

"Will my devices work in my destination country?"

The answer should be understandable within five seconds.

------------------------------------------------------------

RESULT PAGE

The comparison result should always begin with:

🇬🇧 United Kingdom → 🇯🇵 Japan

Large recommendation:

🟢 No adapter required

or

🟡 Plug adapter required

or

🔴 Voltage converter required

Immediately underneath:

Explain WHY using plain English.

Example:

"Your UK plug will not fit Japanese sockets.

Most modern phone and laptop chargers will still work safely.

Some high-powered appliances such as hair dryers may require a voltage converter."

Technical information should appear BELOW inside expandable sections.

Never force users to understand plug types before receiving the answer.

------------------------------------------------------------

DEVICE EXPERIENCE

The user should immediately understand whether their devices work.

Display cards for:

📱 Phone

💻 Laptop

⌚ Smartwatch

📷 Camera

🪥 Electric toothbrush

🔥 Hair dryer

💇 Hair straightener

🎮 Gaming console

Each card should clearly show:

✅ Works

🟡 Adapter needed

🔴 Converter needed

⚠ Check label

Never use technical wording unless necessary.

------------------------------------------------------------

HOMEPAGE

The homepage should feel calm.

Large headline.

Minimal interface.

Comparison tool above the fold.

Suggested headline:

Know if your charger will work before you fly.

Suggested subheading:

Compare any two countries and instantly see if you need a plug adapter, a voltage converter, or nothing at all.

Everything else is secondary.

------------------------------------------------------------

INTERACTIVE GLOBE

Do NOT build the globe yet.

Instead:

Prepare the architecture.

Design the future experience.

Create placeholders where appropriate.

The future globe should allow users to:

- rotate Earth
- hover countries
- click countries
- instantly view socket information

The globe should become an Explore feature rather than replacing search.
